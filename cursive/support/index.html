Cursive Writing for Kids — Support

Last updated: 1 October 2026

What the app does

Cursive Writing for Kids teaches joined-up handwriting one stroke at a time. A green dot shows where each stroke starts, an arrow shows the direction, and “Show me” replays the stroke. Children trace with a finger or Apple Pencil and earn up to three stars per letter. It covers all 52 letters, 38 practice words and printable US Letter worksheets. It is a one-time purchase with no ads, subscriptions or in-app purchases, and works offline.

I'm stuck on a letter

After a few tries the app lets your child move on, so nobody gets stuck. A grown-up can switch between Relaxed and Precise tracing in settings.

How do I reach the grown-up area?

Tap the “Grown-ups” button on the home screen. You will be asked a multiplication question written in words, for example “What is twenty-four times three?”. Type the answer as digits (72). After three wrong answers the gate locks for 30 seconds. Sharing worksheets, the rating prompt, resetting stars and settings are behind it.

Printing worksheets

From the grown-up area, share a worksheet and print it from the share sheet. Worksheets are US Letter size with a model, dotted letters to trace and blank practice lines.

The letter shapes look different from my child's class

The glyphs are original hand-drawn stroke data in a common modern school cursive style. Schools and curricula differ, so shapes may vary slightly from your child's classroom model.

Progress disappeared

Progress lives only on this device. Because there is no server and no account, support cannot recover anything for you — there is no copy anywhere else. Use your device backups when changing devices.

Getting help

Email support@makunch.com. Please include your device model and iOS version, and describe what you expected to happen alongside what actually happened.
