Cursive Writing for Kids — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Cursive Writing for Kids collects no data. It has no user account, contacts no server, and contains no analytics, advertising or tracking software of any kind.

What is collected

Nothing. The app has no networking code, so there is no mechanism by which information could be sent anywhere, and no third-party software development kit is bundled with it. It works fully offline.

What stays on your device

Stars and progress are stored in a single file inside the app's own container, and settings stay on the device. Nothing is transmitted. Deleting the app removes it. The app does not use the camera, microphone, photo library or contacts.

Worksheets

Printable worksheets are rendered on the device. They leave the device only when a grown-up chooses to share or print one through the system share sheet.

Tracking

None. The app does not use the Advertising Identifier, does not fingerprint your device, and is not part of any advertising network. Nothing is shared with data brokers because nothing is gathered in the first place.

Children

This app is made for children aged 6 to 8 and is in the Kids category. Because nothing is collected from anyone, nothing is collected from children. There are no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics and no third-party SDKs. Sharing, the rating prompt, resetting stars and settings are behind a parental gate: a multiplication question written in words.

Changes

If a future version ever collects anything, this page will say so before that version ships, and the App Store privacy label will be updated to match.
