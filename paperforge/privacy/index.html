Paper Studio — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 August 2026

Paper Studio collects no data. It has no user account, contacts no server, and contains no analytics, advertising or tracking software of any kind.

What is collected

Nothing. The app has no networking code, so there is no mechanism by which information could be sent anywhere, and no third-party software development kit is bundled with it.

What you create stays on your device

Templates and presets you create are stored in the app's own container on your device. Exported PDFs go wherever you choose to save or share them. Deleting the app removes it.

Tracking

None. Paper Studio does not use the Advertising Identifier, does not fingerprint your device, and is not part of any advertising network. Nothing is shared with data brokers because nothing is gathered in the first place.

Children

Because nothing is collected from anyone, nothing is collected from children.

Changes

If a future version of Paper Studio ever collects anything, this page will say so before that version ships, and the App Store privacy label will be updated to match.
