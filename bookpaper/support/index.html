BookPaper — Support

Last updated: 15 August 2026

Getting help

Email support@makunch.com. Please include your device model and iOS version, and describe what you expected to happen alongside what actually happened.

Your data

Photographs are never uploaded, and exported images leave the device only when you share them yourself. Because BookPaper has no server and no account, support cannot recover anything for you — there is no copy anywhere else. Keep your own backups.

Privacy

See the privacy policy. In short: nothing is collected.
